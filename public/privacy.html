Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Garden (“we”) provides a marketplace for groceries and delivery in Tanzania. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and mobile apps.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email, phone)
  • Order, delivery address, and payment-method choices
  • Vendor and driver application details when you apply as a partner
  • Support chat messages you send to Garden Support
  • Basic device and usage data needed to keep the service secure

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process orders, delivery, payouts, and customer support
  • To prevent fraud and keep the platform safe
  • To improve the product

Sharing

We share only what is needed with vendors and drivers fulfilling your order, and with payment or hosting providers that help us operate Garden. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention & security

We keep account and order records as long as needed for operations, legal, and accounting requirements. Access is limited to authorized Garden systems and staff.

Your choices

You can update profile details in your account. To request deletion or ask privacy questions, contact us through in-app support or email the address listed on the Garden site.

Children

Garden is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above when we do.
